Super Thinking, mapped

The 59 mental models from Weinberg & McCann’s book, grouped by its nine chapters. Tap a model on the map to open its visual action plan, with real-world examples from many fields.

Summaries, steps and examples are my own short paraphrases, written for quick recall. The book has the full explanations. Some historical examples are well-known stories whose details are debated; they’re here to make the idea stick.
